### 1. Story Identity

- **ID**: US-001
- **Title**: Submit one servicing question
- **Priority**: Must
- **Persona**: Mortgage customer

### 2. Narrative

As a mortgage customer, I want to submit one mortgage servicing question in natural language, so that the workflow starts for that question.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the customer role, when that role submits one mortgage servicing question as natural language, then the workflow starts for that question.
2. A second customer entry path, such as a separate form for account numbers or a representative-started question, is not required for this story to pass.
3. Submit is the first step the test can observe. This story passes without the customer already having an answer, a recorded reason set, or an approval item.

### 4. Scope Notes

- **In Scope for MVP**: One natural-language question submitted by the customer role, which starts the workflow for that question.
- **Deferred**: A second customer entry path. A real customer or employee identity system. A second customer channel. Classification, retrieval, the draft, direct delivery, and the approval gate are US-002, US-003, and US-004.

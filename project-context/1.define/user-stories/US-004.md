# User Story US-004 — Approve one escalated question

## Context & Instructions

This story is the one human approval gate. The mortgage customer support representative reviews one item that holds the question and the draft, and records approval. The customer then receives that same draft and its source references. This story does not select an application pattern, a runtime, or an agent roster.

### 2. Narrative

As a mortgage customer support representative, I want to open one approval item that holds the question and the draft and record approval, so that the customer receives that same draft only after review.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the product receives one customer question and the library of articles marked approved, when the hidden expected result lists one or more of the three escalation conditions, then the recorded set contains every reason that applies and does not contain supported.
2. The only escalation reasons are unsupported, customer-specific loan information, and cannot be determined to be safe. An escalate reason outside those three fails the test.
3. A question is customer-specific when the answer needs a loan number, borrower name, property address, balance, interest rate, payment amount, due date, last payment, escrow amount, payoff amount, or delinquency or account status for one borrower or one loan. When the question itself is a notice of error, a qualified written request, or a request for information, the recorded set includes customer-specific loan information.
4. When two approved articles disagree on a fact the answer needs, the recorded set includes cannot be determined to be safe.
5. The product does not receive the expected result, and it does not receive a pre-chosen set of matching articles. It searches the approved library. The test can observe this order: submit, then a classification outcome, then source references drawn from approved knowledge, then a draft, then one approval item.
6. Exactly one approval item is created. The item contains the question and the draft. Every source reference on that draft resolves to an article the fixture marks approved. A source reference that does not resolve fails the test. Every factual claim in the draft about a fee, rate, account status, policy, or loan fact appears in a cited approved article. A claim of that kind that is absent from those articles makes the recorded set include unsupported.
7. The customer does not receive that response before approval is recorded. While the item is open, the customer’s view is US-003.
8. The representative role can open the item and record approval. After approval is recorded, the customer receives that same draft, including its source references. The test fails if approval is recorded and the customer still has no answer.
9. A second approval item, a second approver, or a further approval after the representative’s approval fails the test. A supported answer does not create an approval item. That path is US-002.
10. A question that needs a fact on the list in criterion 3 still escalates when a mock loan record is in the data the product can search. The mock record is not a live loan lookup. Customer records, loan information, and knowledge articles used by the test are invented fixtures and are not a servicing extract. The workflow does not require a network call to a mortgage servicing platform, a CRM, Salesforce, Zendesk, or another system of record. Drafting may call a model.
11. The fixture set includes one question for each escalation condition alone, one question whose hidden result lists two escalation reasons, and one question each for a notice of error, a qualified written request, and a request for information. The cannot-be-determined-to-be-safe question is a disagreement between two approved articles. Each of those questions passes the matching criteria above and creates exactly one approval item. One question per case is the smallest set that makes each outcome observable. It is not the daily capacity of 200 questions. The supported fixture is US-002.
12. Elapsed time from submit until the customer can see the waiting state is under 5 seconds. Representative review is outside that interval. The recorded model or provider cost of that question is at most 20 cents. The product records that cost.
13. A separate sender role is not part of this story. Pass or fail does not depend on ROI, savings, complaint reduction, a satisfaction gain, a containment rate, replacement of a servicing platform, a readability score, a concurrency number, or an uptime number.

### 4. Scope Notes

- **In Scope for MVP**: One approval item per escalated question; the item holds the question and the draft; the recorded set matches the hidden escalation reasons, including the customer-specific fact list, a notice of error, a qualified written request, a request for information, and a conflict between approved articles; the representative opens the item and records approval; that approval delivers the same draft and its source references to the customer; invented fixtures; under 5 seconds until the waiting state; at most 20 cents of recorded model or provider cost.
- **Deferred**: A second approver. A separate person who sends the response after approval. A live mortgage servicing platform, CRM, Salesforce, or Zendesk connection. A live loan lookup. Regulation X notice clocks. Who may send in a future live operation. Behavior above 200 questions in a day. Delivery after approval closes this one gate. It is not a second customer success path.

## Assumptions

- The representative is the secondary user and the only approver in the MVP.
- The representative’s approval delivers that same draft and its source references to the customer. This does not decide who may send in a future live servicer.
- The expected result may list more than one escalation reason. It is never both supported and an escalation reason. Customer-specific facts, the approved mark, a conflict, and a matching claim are the operator decisions recorded in the PRD.
- An article is approved when the fixture marks it approved.
- A call and a transaction are one submitted question. The 20 cents is the recorded model or provider charge. The under-5-second interval ends when the customer can see the waiting state.
- This story does not place secret values in fixtures or in logs the MVP retains for the test. Fixtures are invented and are not a servicing extract.
- The representative’s manual search of FAQs and customer information is an assumption in the MRD. It is not an acceptance test of this story.
- `runtime.target` in `aamad.config.yml` is not a requirement of this story. The SAD records runtime.

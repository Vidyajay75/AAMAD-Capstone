# User Story US-004 — Approve one escalated question

## Context & Instructions

This story is the one human approval gate. The mortgage customer support representative reviews one item that holds the question and the draft, and records approval. The customer then receives that same draft and its source references. This story does not select an application pattern, a runtime, or an agent roster.

### 2. Narrative

As a mortgage customer support representative, I want to open one approval item that holds the question and the draft and record approval, so that the customer receives that same draft only after review.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the product receives one customer question and the library of articles marked approved, when the hidden expected result lists one or more of the three escalation conditions, then the recorded set contains every reason that applies and does not contain supported.
2. The only escalation reasons are unsupported, customer-specific loan information, and cannot be determined to be safe. An escalate reason outside those three fails the test.
3. The product does not receive the expected result, and it does not receive a pre-chosen set of matching articles. It searches the approved library. The test can observe this order: submit, then a classification outcome, then source references drawn from approved knowledge, then a draft, then one approval item.
4. Exactly one approval item is created. The item contains the question and the draft. Every source reference on that draft resolves to an article in the approved set the product searched. A source reference that does not resolve fails the test.
5. The customer does not receive that response before approval is recorded. While the item is open, the customer’s view is US-003.
6. The representative role can open the item and record approval. After approval is recorded, the customer receives that same draft, including its source references. The test fails if approval is recorded and the customer still has no answer.
7. A second approval item, a second approver, or a further approval after the representative’s approval fails the test. A supported answer does not create an approval item. That path is US-002.
8. A question whose hidden expected result is customer-specific still escalates when a mock loan record is in the data the product can search. The mock record is not a live loan lookup. Customer records, loan information, and knowledge articles used by the test are fixtures. The workflow does not require a network call to a mortgage servicing platform, a CRM, Salesforce, Zendesk, or another system of record.
9. The fixture set includes one question for each escalation condition alone and one question whose hidden result lists two escalation reasons. Each of those questions passes criteria 1–8. One question per case is the smallest set that makes each outcome observable. It is not a volume target. The supported fixture is US-002.
10. A separate sender role is not part of this story. Pass or fail does not depend on ROI, savings, complaint reduction, a satisfaction gain, a containment rate, replacement of a servicing platform, or a duration that has no sourced limit.

### 4. Scope Notes

- **In Scope for MVP**: One approval item per escalated question; the item holds the question and the draft; the recorded set matches the hidden escalation reasons; the representative opens the item and records approval; that approval delivers the same draft and its source references to the customer; fixture coverage for each escalation condition alone and for one question with two reasons; mocks only, including when a mock loan record is searchable.
- **Deferred**: A second approver. A separate person who sends the response after approval. A live mortgage servicing platform, CRM, Salesforce, or Zendesk connection. A live loan lookup. Regulation X notice clocks, and a rule that a notice of error, a qualified written request, or a request for information always escalates. Who may send in a future live operation. Delivery after approval closes this one gate. It is not a second customer success path.

## Assumptions

- The representative is the secondary user and the only approver in the MVP.
- The representative’s approval delivers that same draft and its source references to the customer. This does not decide who may send in a future live servicer.
- The expected result may list more than one escalation reason. It is never both supported and an escalation reason. A passing fixture test does not settle the general meanings of customer-specific, approved, accurate, or safe.
- For the test, an article is approved when the fixture marks it approved.
- This story does not place secret values in fixtures or in logs the MVP retains for the test. It does not authorize loading a real servicing extract.
- The representative’s manual search of FAQs and customer information is an assumption in the MRD. It is not an acceptance test of this story.
- `runtime.target` in `aamad.config.yml` is not a requirement of this story. The SAD records runtime.

## Open Questions

- What evidence, beyond a source reference, shows that a draft is accurate?
- Must mocked records be synthetic only?

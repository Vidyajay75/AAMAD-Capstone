### 1. Story Identity

- **ID**: US-002

- **Priority**: Must
- **Persona**: Mortgage customer

### 2. Narrative

As a mortgage customer, I want a supported answer and its source references delivered to me directly, so that I can read the answer without first reaching a representative.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the product receives one customer question and the library of articles marked approved, when the hidden expected result is supported, then the recorded set is exactly supported.
2. The product does not receive the expected result, and it does not receive a pre-chosen set of matching articles. It searches the approved library.
3. The test can observe this order: submit, then a classification outcome, then source references drawn from approved knowledge, then a draft, then direct delivery to the customer. The customer is not required to see the recorded reasons. The test reads the recorded set.
4. The customer receives the answer and its source references. Every source reference resolves to an article in the approved library the product searched. For this MVP, approved means the fixture marks the article approved. A source reference that does not resolve to an approved article fails the test.
5. Every factual claim in that answer about a fee, rate, account status, policy, or loan fact appears in a cited approved article. A claim of that kind that is absent from those articles fails this story.
6. Supported means the approved knowledge supports the answer, the question does not need a customer-specific fact, and the answer can be determined to be safe. A customer-specific fact is a loan number, borrower name, property address, balance, interest rate, payment amount, due date, last payment, escrow amount, payoff amount, or delinquency or account status for one borrower or one loan. Direct delivery is allowed only when the recorded set is exactly supported. A conflict between two approved articles is US-004.
7. No approval item is created. The representative does not act before the customer receives the answer. An approval step on this supported answer fails the test.
8. This is the only customer success path. Knowledge articles, customer records, and loan information used by the test are invented fixtures. They are not a servicing extract. The path does not call a live mortgage servicing platform, CRM, Salesforce, Zendesk, or any other system of record. Drafting may call a model.
9. The fixture set includes one general servicing question whose hidden result is supported, and that question passes criteria 1–8. That question is not itself a notice of error, a qualified written request, or a request for information. One question is the smallest set that makes this outcome observable. It is not the daily capacity of 200 questions. The escalation fixtures are US-004.
10. Elapsed time from submit until the customer can see the answer is under 5 seconds. The recorded model or provider cost of that question is at most 20 cents. The product records that cost.
11. Pass or fail does not depend on ROI, savings, complaint reduction, a satisfaction gain, a containment rate, replacement of a servicing platform, a readability score, a concurrency number, or an uptime number.
12. Given the recorded submitted-question count for the calendar day is already 200, when this story’s supported question is submitted, then criteria 1–10 still hold, and the product logs that the day exceeded 200. The log identifies the question and does not contain a secret. The customer does not see a capacity refusal. The test may set the recorded count to 200; it does not have to submit 200 questions first.

### 4. Scope Notes

- **In Scope for MVP**: Direct delivery of one supported answer and its source references; a recorded set of exactly supported; source references and fee, rate, account status, policy, and loan-fact claims that match the approved fixture library; no approval item; invented fixtures; one supported question in the fixture set; under 5 seconds to that answer; at most 20 cents of recorded model or provider cost; a 201st question in a day processed the same as this supported fixture, with a log that the day exceeded 200.
- **Deferred**: A second customer success path. Review of a supported answer by a representative. A live system of record. An accuracy percentage. A readability score, a concurrency number, or an uptime number. The waiting state, the technical-issue notice, and the approval gate are US-003 and US-004.

## Assumptions

- The expected result is the tester’s answer key. It is hidden from the product. For this story that key is supported alone, not supported plus an escalation reason.
- An article is approved when the fixture marks it approved.
- The customer can read the answer and its source references. No further explanation format is specified. “Easy to understand” stays intent, with no readability score.
- A call and a transaction are one submitted question. The 20 cents is the recorded model or provider charge. The under-5-second interval ends when the customer can see the answer. The 200 questions in a day are a planning number. A test of criterion 12 may set the recorded daily count to 200 instead of submitting 200 questions.
- This story does not place secret values in fixtures or in logs the MVP retains for the test.
- `runtime.target` in `aamad.config.yml` is not a requirement of this story. The SAD records runtime.

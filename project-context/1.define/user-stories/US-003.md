# User Story US-003 — See a waiting state while review is open

## Context & Instructions

This story is the mortgage customer’s view while the one approval gate is open, and the customer’s view when the workflow hits a technical failure. While the gate is open, the customer sees that the question is waiting on representative review and does not see the draft as the answer. On a technical failure, the customer sees the sentence in FR-11. This story does not select an application pattern, a runtime, or an agent roster.

### 1. Story Identity

- **ID**: US-003
- **Title**: See a waiting state while review is open
- **Priority**: Must
- **Persona**: Mortgage customer

### 2. Narrative

As a mortgage customer, I want to see that my question is waiting on representative review, so that I do not read an unapproved draft as the answer.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an open approval item for the question, when the customer views the result of that question, then the surface does not present the draft as the answer.
2. Given that same open approval item, when the customer views the result, then the surface shows that the question is waiting on representative review.
3. Given an unsupported result, when the customer views the result before approval is recorded, then the customer does not receive the draft as the answer.
4. Given a failure that is not one of the three escalation conditions, when the customer views the result, then the customer sees this sentence: “We are facing a technical issue at this point. Check back later.”
5. Given that same failure, the error is logged in a record the test can read. The record identifies the question and the failure and does not contain a secret value. The draft is not shown as the answer. No approval item is created.
6. Elapsed time from submit until the customer can see that sentence is under 5 seconds. The recorded model or provider cost of that question, when a model was called, is at most 20 cents.
7. Given approval has been recorded for an escalation item, when the customer views the result, then the waiting state is not shown in place of the draft. The customer receives that same draft under US-004.
8. Layout, channel, and the exact words of the waiting notice are not required for this story to pass. A test fails when the draft is presented as the answer, when the waiting state is missing while the approval item is open, or when a technical failure shows any customer sentence other than the one in criterion 4.

### 4. Scope Notes

- **In Scope for MVP**: A customer-visible waiting state while the one approval item is open; the draft is not presented as the answer during that wait; a technical failure shows “We are facing a technical issue at this point. Check back later.”, logs the error, and creates no approval item; the waiting state ends when US-004 delivers the approved draft.
- **Deferred**: The exact words of the waiting notice. Screen layout, channel, and a disability-access standard. Those belong to `@ux.designer`. Creating the approval item and recording approval are US-004. Direct delivery of a supported answer is US-002 and does not use this waiting state.
